Annotations

1 Typed Send-to
Findings are {category,key,value,source,severity,meta} (eZrecon's shape). The row action names the destination; nothing is free-text.
2 Instrumented for free
CICS primitives are macros on the existing session, so MITM, Traffic Recorder and Anomaly Detector see every 3270 write with no extra wiring.
3 Fingerprint reconcile
Recon's external guess and the in-session ESM verdict land on the same Target and are shown together. Before + during is a capability hack3270 doesn't pair.
4 Clean-room, not embed
Four primitives (~350 lines) on the tn3270 stack. GPL CICSpwn stays as the conformance oracle, never linked into the bridge.
5 Single record
Recon findings, session recordings, CICS actions and the ESM trail write into one Engagement. The report generator has one input.
